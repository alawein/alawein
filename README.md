My background in computational quantum physics and materials science shapes how I approach mathematical modeling, simulation, and the validation of computational results.

## Engineering approach

- Make assumptions and acceptance criteria explicit.
- Check numerical behavior separately from physical interpretation.
- Build reproducible workflows with inspectable inputs, outputs, and failure modes.

## Collaborate

For research and engineering collaboration, bring a question about an AI system, a mathematical model, or a computational workflow. For a tool-specific issue, include the input, expected behavior, and observed result in its repository.
