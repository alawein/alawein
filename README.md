# Meshal Alawein

I'm Meshal Alawein, an AI systems engineer and computational scientist. My current work sits at the intersection of AI and scientific computing, including scientific machine learning and agent evaluation.

My research background is in computational quantum physics and materials science. I use mathematical modeling, simulation, and software engineering to explore problems and test the assumptions behind their results.

## Selected tools

- [Outcome Check](https://github.com/alawein/outcome-check): compare explicit requirements with supplied state observations. Keep action status separate from confirmed, contradicted, and unobserved outcomes.
- [Eval Audit](https://github.com/alawein/eval-audit): find missing, errored, unscored, and unexpected evaluation records against a declared population before interpreting scores.
- [Claim Review](https://github.com/alawein/claim-review): record human judgments against exact source passages and export the review history. Runs in the browser without a server or model.

## Selected research

I hold a Ph.D. in EECS from UC Berkeley. My research includes computational materials science at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory and first-author work in Physical Review Materials on hole localization and flat bands in rippled MoS2 monolayers.

- [Strain-induced lateral heterostructures: Hole localization and the emergence of flat bands in rippled MoS2 monolayers](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevMaterials.9.L021002), Physical Review Materials, 2025
- [Heteroepitaxial beta-Ga2O3 on Conductive Ceramic Templates: Toward Ultrahigh Gain Deep-Ultraviolet Photodetection](https://doi.org/10.1002/admt.202100142), Advanced Materials Technologies, 2021
- [Time-energy quantum uncertainty: Quantifying the effectiveness of surface defect passivation protocols for low-dimensional semiconductors](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsaelm.9b00578), ACS Applied Electronic Materials, 2020
- Multistate nanomagnetic logic using equilateral permalloy triangles, IEEE Magnetics Letters, 2019
